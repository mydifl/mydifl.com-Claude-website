A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 (DELF) C1 · C2 (DALF) Valid for Life French Govt. Diploma

Current DELF/DALF calendar

Dates, registration windows and candidate fees are published for each current session by the authorised Alliance Française or Institut français. Check the current official calendar and fee table before registering.

Authorised centre check

Candidate category, level, centre and session can affect the fee. Use the selected centre's current written fee and cancellation terms.

Official Links

Why DELF/DALF Matters

  • DELF B2 — Commonly requested by French-taught university programmes; each institution sets its own rule
  • DELF B1 — May support some nationality or residency applications; verify the current official rule
  • DALF C1 — Demonstrates advanced French; confirm the receiving institution's accepted qualifications
  • The diploma itself does not expire, but an institution may ask for recent evidence of ability
