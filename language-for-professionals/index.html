Japanese for Jaipur's Professionals

Japanese may help professionals communicate with Japanese-speaking buyers, visitors, clients or teams. Employment and pay depend on the role, employer, domain skills, experience and current market; no certificate guarantees a job or salary premium.

Certification: JLPT N5 to N1 · NAT Exam. Learn Japanese at DIFL →

French for Canada, Commerce & Culture

Qualifying TEF Canada or TCF Canada results may affect an Express Entry profile under current IRCC rules, while other Canadian programmes set their own evidence. French can also support professional communication with Francophone customers and partners.

Certification: DELF A1–B2 · DALF C1–C2 · TEF Canada · TCF Canada. Learn French at DIFL →

Mandarin Chinese — Jaipur's Trade Language

Mandarin may support direct supplier, product and trade-fair communication. Commercial outcomes depend on proficiency, contracts, market conditions and professional advice; learning Mandarin does not guarantee savings or eliminate the need for qualified intermediaries.

Certification: HSK 1–6 · HSKK. Learn Mandarin at DIFL →

Italian — Marble, Migration & Milan

Italian can support workplace, daily-life and trade communication. Decreto Flussi is a quota-based process without a universal CILS level; employer, sector and official route rules vary. DIFL does not promise employment, a visa or a margin increase.

Certification: CILS A1–C2 · PLIDA. Learn Italian at DIFL →

Korean — From Jaipur to Seoul

EPS-TOPIK is a Korean-language assessment used in parts of Korea's Employment Permit System, but recruitment, skills, eligibility, employer and visa requirements also apply. GKS benefits, participating universities and TOPIK expectations vary by track and annual notice. Passing a test or completing coaching does not guarantee selection, employment, a scholarship or a visa; confirm every current requirement with the responsible official authority.

Preparation: TOPIK I & II · general EPS-TOPIK overview subject to sending-country eligibility. Learn Korean at DIFL →
