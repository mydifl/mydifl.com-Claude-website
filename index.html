International Programs

Beyond language learning, DIFL provides language preparation and general informational guidance for selected study, vocational and scholarship pathways. Institutions, employers and public authorities set the official requirements and decide every admission, employment and visa outcome; DIFL does not provide legal or immigration advice.

🏭
🇩🇪

Ausbildung — Germany

Vocational Training Program

Ausbildung (Duale Ausbildung) is Germany's dual vocational-training system, combining school learning with employer-based training. Length varies by occupation; successful completion can result in the qualification specified by the applicable training rules. Sectors and vacancies vary over time.

  • German expectations vary by occupation, employer, school and visa route; B1 is commonly requested
  • Training allowances vary by occupation, employer, region and training year
  • Residence and later settlement options are conditional on current German law and the applicant's circumstances
  • Applicants must meet the employer, training-school and visa requirements for their chosen occupation
  • DIFL prepares you for Goethe/TELC A1 to B2 certification
  • General documentation guidance is available; official authorities decide visa applications
⛩️
🇯🇵

MEXT Scholarship — Japan

Japanese Government Scholarship

The MEXT (Monbukagakusho) Scholarship supports eligible international students in several study categories. Benefits, academic criteria and language expectations vary by category and year. DIFL provides Japanese-language preparation; applicants should confirm current requirements on the official Study in Japan website.

  • Benefits may include tuition, an allowance and travel support; amounts and eligibility vary by category and current notice
  • Programs: Research Students, Undergraduate, College of Technology
  • Language expectations and selection criteria vary by scholarship category
  • DIFL prepares JLPT N5 through N1 — all levels covered
  • Application support: SOP, document prep, interview coaching
  • Programme duration and preparatory study depend on the selected category and official offer
🏫
🇫🇷

Campus France — France

Study in France Program

Campus France is the French public service for international student mobility. Language evidence depends on the institution and program language; DIFL helps learners prepare their French while applicants confirm the exact requirement with the university and official Campus France guidance.

  • Official information and application support for many France study pathways
  • DELF/DALF A1–C2 preparation offered at DIFL
  • TEF Canada / TCF Canada for French immigration
  • Tuition and living costs vary by institution, programme, city and applicant status
  • DIFL covers all levels from beginner to C2 proficiency
  • Application-document guidance; institutions and authorities make admission and visa decisions
🌸
🇰🇷

GKS — Korean Government Scholarship

Global Korea Scholarship

The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) supports eligible international students. Academic, language and application requirements vary by track and annual notice. Korean ability and TOPIK results may strengthen some applications; always check the current official GKS notice.

  • Benefits and financial support vary by track and annual notice
  • Language-training arrangements vary by track, prior proficiency and current scholarship terms
  • TOPIK Level 1–6 — DIFL prepares all levels
  • Participating universities may include major Korean institutions; verify the current official university list
  • Undergraduate and postgraduate programs available
  • DIFL provides TOPIK exam preparation and application support
🍁
🇨🇦

Canada Immigration — French (TEF/TCF)

Immigration Language Program

IRCC accepts TEF Canada and TCF Canada results for Express Entry, subject to current score and validity rules. Other federal, provincial and Québec programmes set their own language evidence. DIFL provides language-test preparation, not immigration advice or an invitation guarantee.

  • TEF Canada / TCF Canada preparation — all 4 skills
  • Qualifying French and English results may add CRS points under current Express Entry criteria
  • Programme-specific language evidence must be confirmed with the responsible authority
  • French teaching at DIFL is supported by an institute history dating to 1970
  • Courses from A1 to C1 — matched to your immigration goals
  • Official-source guidance on checking current test requirements
🏥
🇩🇪

Ausbildung Nursing — Germany

Healthcare Vocational Program

Germany's nursing vocational-training pathway (Pflegeausbildung) combines school-based learning with employer-based practical training. Language, recognition, employer and visa requirements vary, so applicants should confirm the current rules with official German authorities and the relevant training provider.

  • German expectations commonly begin around B1 but vary by employer, school and authority
  • Training allowances vary by employer, region and training year
  • Where recognition is required, applicants must apply to the competent German authority; requirements and outcomes vary by occupation, state and individual qualification
  • DIFL provides German-language preparation relevant to healthcare communication
  • Goethe Institut and TELC exam preparation included
  • Medical German vocabulary and healthcare communication training

V K Sharma Memorial Scholarship

Named in lifelong honour of DIFL's founder, Late Dr. V K Sharma — this scholarship provides meaningful financial support to deserving students who wish to learn a foreign language but face genuine hardship. Applications are reviewed personally and with utmost care by Director Priyanshu Sharma.

⚠️

This internal DIFL fee-support scholarship carries strict prerequisites. Acceptance is not guaranteed and review time varies. Access is limited to authorised staff, but no transmission method can promise absolute secrecy.

Protect sensitive documents: begin with a call or brief message only. Do not send original identity, income, service, retirement, disability or medical documents through an ordinary website form, email or unsolicited WhatsApp message. An authorised DIFL staff member will explain the approved submission method and which details may be safely redacted before a copy is requested. See the scholarship privacy notice.
Who Is Eligible
🎖️

Retired Armed Forces / Retired Police Families

Children or self of Retired Armed Forces personnel (Army, Navy, Air Force, Paramilitary) or Retired Police officers. Documentary proof of service and retirement required. India's defenders and their families deserve every opportunity to grow.

📎 Required: Discharge / Retirement certificate, service record, relationship proof
🤝

Below Poverty Line (BPL) Families

Students from families that can demonstrate genuine financial need. BPL card or equivalent government-issued documentation required. DIFL is committed to ensuring that economic circumstance is never a barrier to quality language education.

📎 Required: BPL ration card / income certificate issued by competent authority / supporting financial documentation
♿

Persons with Disabilities

Students with a recognised physical or other disability. DIFL believes language opens the world for everyone — including those who face additional challenges in their daily lives. DIFL is proud to be an inclusive institution.

📎 Required: Disability certificate issued by a government medical authority
